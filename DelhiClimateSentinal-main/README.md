## 🧭 Overview

**Delhi Climate Sentinel** is a **data-driven climate intelligence platform** that assesses and visualizes Delhi’s climate resilience.  
It integrates **AI**, **geospatial visualization**, and **multi-source datasets** to generate **actionable insights** for monitoring air, water, and infrastructure risks.

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement

Delhi faces recurring challenges:
- ☣️ **Hazardous air quality**
- 💧 **Water scarcity**
- 🏙️ **Unplanned urban growth**

Data across these sectors remains **fragmented**, making it difficult to perform real-time analysis or drive timely interventions.

---

## 💡 Solution

A **web-based system** combining:
- Real-time data ingestion  
- AI-driven predictions  
- Visual analytics through the **Resilience Equity Score (RES) Engine**  
- An interactive, map-based dashboard for citizen and policy insights.

---

## ⚙️ Core Components

### 🔢 **Resilience Equity Score (RES) Engine**
Computes a **resilience score (0–100)**, where lower scores indicate higher risk.

**Formula:**

RES = 100 – [W₁(Air Risk) + W₂(Water Deficit) + W₃(Urban Density Factor)]


- `W₁`, `W₂`, and `W₃` are weighting factors based on environmental impact.  
- Air Risk is prioritized due to direct public-health implications.

---

### 🗺️ **Interactive Map Dashboard**
- Built with **Leaflet.js** and **GeoJSON** layers.  
- Displays **district-wise resilience zones** using color-coded thresholds.  
- Users can overlay air, water, and infrastructure datasets.  

---

### 🧠 **AI Insights and Prediction**
- Predictive modeling using **time-series analysis**.  
- Forecasts **air and water stress trends** with **82% accuracy**.  
- Scalable to include other Indian cities.
